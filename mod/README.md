# Cool Link Shades — experimental

Head-bound sunglasses for Adult Link in Ocarina of Time remake update 2.0.0, title ID `0400486024DEC000`.

Close Lichii and copy this folder to `%APPDATA%\eden\load\0400486024DEC000\Cool Link Shades (experimental)`, then enable it in the game's add-ons. Close Lichii and disable or remove that folder to uninstall.

The mod replaces `romfs/Model/Player_Adult.Player_Adult.bfres.zs` and the three `Mt_Boots_Player_Adult` albedo, normal and roughness textures under `romfs/Tex/`. It conflicts with other mods replacing those files; gameplay logic and saves are unchanged.

The green outfit and glasses were checked in an isolated Lichii equipment view, and the glasses appeared in a dark dungeon gameplay view. Rolling, outdoor lighting, cutscenes, other equipment and distant rendering still need testing. Hair may overlap the glasses, and the bridge or sides may be too light in other lighting. Smaller texture mips have not been edited.
