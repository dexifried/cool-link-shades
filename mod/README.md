# Cool Link Shades — Hover Boots update

Head-bound sunglasses for Adult Link in the Ocarina of Time remake, update 2.0.0, title ID `0400486024DEC000`.

Close Lichii and replace the previous `Cool Link Shades (experimental)` folder under `%APPDATA%\eden\load\0400486024DEC000\` with this folder. Enable it in the game's add-ons. Some portable profiles use their own `user/load/0400486024DEC000/` directory. Close Lichii and disable or remove this folder to uninstall.

The mod replaces `romfs/Model/Player_Adult.Player_Adult.bfres.zs` and the three `Mt_Boots_Player_Adult` albedo, normal, and roughness textures under `romfs/Tex/`. It conflicts with other mods replacing those files. It does not alter gameplay logic or saves.

Tested in isolated Lichii build 177: Hover Boots with green and blue tunics and Iron Boots with the green tunic in the equipment view. The previous release was reported working outdoors and in cutscenes; those have not been rechecked in this revision. Rolling, other equipment, and distant rendering need further testing. Hair may overlap the glasses, and the bridge or sides may appear too bright in some lighting. Smaller texture mips have not been edited.
